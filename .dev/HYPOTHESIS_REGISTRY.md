## Result-state vocabulary

Allowed states include `untestable_with_current_evidence`,
`insufficient_sample`, `exploratory_association`,
`no_evidence_of_improved_predictive_value`,
`evidence_supporting_registered_prediction`,
`evidence_inconsistent_with_registered_prediction`, and
`independent_replication_required`.

A negative or untestable result is a completed scientific outcome; it is
not deleted or recoloured.
