# PRODUCT.md — Big Cycle Atlas / Great Power Observatory

## Purpose

Big Cycle Atlas is a personal research instrument for studying the rise,
peak and decline of great powers through the economic and institutional
forces described in Ray Dalio's Big Cycle research. v1.1 ("Great Power
Observatory") re-centres the product on that purpose.

- Signature feature: **the Big Cycle Clock** — where each tracked power
  currently sits on the cycle (experimental composite models).
- Primary research experience: **the 17 Atlas forces**.
- Supporting research: current dominant power, rising challengers,
  historical empire comparisons, relative international power,
  evidence-gated transition forecasting.
- Financial research (markets, strategies, paper portfolios, watchlist)
  remains fully implemented but hidden from the default interface.

## Hard product truths (never violate)

- Classifications are **experimental model outputs**, honestly labelled.
- **No countdown to empire change.** No fabricated forecast probabilities.
- Evidence gates must render gate failures verbatim, not be papered over.
- Scores are model outputs, not investment advice.
- 9 forces publish numerical levels · 2 dimension-only · 6 context profiles.
- All market/paper-portfolio functionality stays in the codebase and
  reachable by URL — it is hidden from default chrome, not deleted.

## Surfaces

- Astro + React islands, ECharts, Lucide. Single deployment: FastAPI serves
  the built frontend natively on Windows (primary), Linux optional.
- Modes: **Operate** (research instrument) with one **Experience** moment
  (the Clock hero).
- Owner: single user, local-first, dark-by-default, light theme supported.

## Visual identity

Owner-supplied logo: thin gold line-art armillary sphere (meridian globe,
triangle apex marker, orbit arc) with "BIG CYCLE ATLAS" in spaced thin
capitals on white. The design world is **celestial cartography / observatory
instrument**: precision instrument for reading the cycle — antique brass and
gold on deep ink in dark mode; gold ink on parchment in light mode.
Liquid Glass is retained for floating chrome.
